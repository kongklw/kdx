# Delivery & Beyond

Article Body
​​​​Giving birth is one of the most extraordinary experiences of a woman’s life. Yet even after months of careful preparation and anticipation, the moment of birth is almost never what you had expected. Your health, the condition of the fetus, and the policies of the hospital will all help determine what actually happens. What matters is that the baby is here at last and healthy.

# Chronic Conditions

**来源**: [https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/chronic/Pages/default.aspx](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/chronic/Pages/default.aspx)

---

Article Body
Ten to twenty million children and adolescents in the United States have some form of chronic illness or disability.
Chronic
refers to a health condition that lasts anywhere from three months to a lifetime.

# Your Child’s Checkups

**来源**: [https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/Your-Childs-Checkups/Pages/default.aspx](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/Your-Childs-Checkups/Pages/default.aspx)

Article Body
​​
Regular checkups
are an important way to keep track of your child's health and development. Your health care team will take measurements, conduct a head-to-toe examination, update immunizations, and answer questions you may have. Be sure to share your successes and milestones, any concerns about your child's development, and challenges in daily routines. These visits are a chance to learn as much as you can about the best ways to help your child grow and thrive!

# Chest & Lungs

**来源**: [https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/chest-lungs/Pages/default.aspx](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/chest-lungs/Pages/default.aspx)

Article Body
Healthy lungs are crucial to your child's well-being. The lungs can get infections that, when serious, can cause chronic and sometimes life-threatening illnesses.

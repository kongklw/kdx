# Bathing & Skin Care

**来源**: [https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/bathing-skin-care/Pages/default.aspx](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/bathing-skin-care/Pages/default.aspx)

---

Article Body
​​​​​Your baby doesn’t need much bathing if you wash the diaper area thoroughly during diaper changes. Three times a week during her first year may be enough. Bathing them more frequently may dry out their skin, particularly if soaps are used or moisture is allowed to evaporate from the skin.

Article Body
At the American Academy of Pediatrics, we only work with companies that share our values: commitment to evidence-based medicine, dedication to the pediatric profession, and the belief that every child matters.
Supporters help us advance our mission, but they do not influence it. The AAP maintains full independence in its policies, content and leadership decisions.

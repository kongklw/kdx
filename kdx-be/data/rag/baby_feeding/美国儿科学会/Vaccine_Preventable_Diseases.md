# Vaccine Preventable Diseases

**来源**: [https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/vaccine-preventable-diseases/Pages/default.aspx](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/vaccine-preventable-diseases/Pages/default.aspx)

Article Body
The use of vaccines has led to major improvements in child health over a relatively short period. Many of the infectious illnesses you or your parents had as children, from chickenpox to polio to measles, no longer affect most children today. By following the
immunization guidelines
recommended
by the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP), you can help make your child healthier than was ever possible in earlier generations. Below, read about some of the diseases that vaccines can prevent.

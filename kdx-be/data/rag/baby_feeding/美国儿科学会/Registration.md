About Our Virtual Parent Panel:
The HealthyChildren.org Virtual Parent Panel is an online community of parents and caregivers interested in children’s health care.
Members of the HealthyChildren.org Virtual Parent Panel receive periodic email invitations to participate in short online surveys and polls designed to ensure the website continues to meet the needs of parents and families. On occasion, participants who complete a survey could be eligible to win a prize.
Participation is optional. It is up to you whether you respond to any specific activity and your contact information and responses will always be kept confidential.​

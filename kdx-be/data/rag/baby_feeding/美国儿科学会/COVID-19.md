# COVID-19

**来源**: [https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/COVID-19/Pages/default.aspx](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/COVID-19/Pages/default.aspx)

---

Article Body
The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends the COVID vaccine for all young children age 6 months through 23 months. Children younger than 2 years old are at highest risk for severe COVID-19 and hospitalization. Beyond that age, it recommends the vaccine for children and teens with risk factors for COVID. The vaccine also should be available for children ages 2-18 who do not fall into these risk groups, but whose parent wants them to have COVID vaccine protection. Learn more here.

# Sports Injuries

**来源**: [https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/sports-injuries/Pages/default.aspx](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/sports-injuries/Pages/default.aspx)

Article Body
Improvements in the quality of protective equipment—such as padding and helmets—have made sports participation safer than ever before. Even so, chil­dren's bodies are still vulnerable to injury.

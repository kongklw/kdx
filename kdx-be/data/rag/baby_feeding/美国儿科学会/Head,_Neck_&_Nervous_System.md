# Head, Neck & Nervous System

**来源**: [https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/head-neck-nervous-system/Pages/default.aspx](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/head-neck-nervous-system/Pages/default.aspx)

Article Body
​During childhood your son or daughter may experience a variety of neurological disorders. Many of these conditions are not cause for concern and go away on their own. For more serious conditions, a
pediatric neurosurgeon can diagnose, treat, and manage children’s nervous system problems and head and spinal deformities.

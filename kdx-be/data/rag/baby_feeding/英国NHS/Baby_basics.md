Baby basics
Becoming a parent can be exciting, challenging and sometimes overwhelming.
Baby basics includes practical tips on everything from changing your first nappy to coping with lack of sleep.
Remember to ask your midwife or health visitor if you need any help or advice.
In this section
Bonding with your baby
Find out how to build a relationship with your baby and understand their needs.
Sleep advice
Find out what your baby needs to sleep safely, how much sleep they need and how to cope with lack of sleep.
Caring for your baby
Tips and advice on bathing and dressing your baby, which vitamins they need, vaccinations and more.
Baby myths and facts
We've broken down some common myths about babies and given you the facts around them.
Help and support
Find out what help and support is available for new parents.
CBeebies Parenting: How can I celebrate my baby’s little victories?
Learn about 'inchstones', the small everyday achievements in your baby's development, and ways to celebrate them.
